Who I Am

About Me

I am an Assistant Professor at PES University, Bangalore, with over a decade of academic experience across several prestigious management institutions in Karnataka. Holding a Ph.D. and MBA, my academic journey has been driven by a passion for making management education impactful, practical, and research-oriented.

My research interests span e-banking, digital payments, financial performance analysis, microfinance, GST, and fintech — with a particular focus on how technology is reshaping the Indian banking and financial landscape.

Beyond teaching, I actively contribute to institutional development through roles as Placement Coordinator, Research Cell member, and Social Internship coordinator. I have served as Women's Wing President and conference convener, and have guided 30+ MBA students through their dissertations.
